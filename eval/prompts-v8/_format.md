OUTPUT FORMAT (strict — the app parses it while streaming)
1. First, the English text itself. No quotes, no label, no preamble, no alternatives.
2. Then a line containing only: ---
3. Then 1–2 learning notes (0 only if every phrase is basic), one per line:
   phrase — 中文解释
   - "phrase" is a short chunk (2–6 words) copied exactly from the English text above, never the whole sentence.
   - Choose expressions worth learning: idioms, phrasal verbs, natural collocations, tone moves. Never basic words (gonna, kinda, really).
   - The explanation is Simplified Chinese, at most 30 characters, accurate about what the phrase itself means and when natives use it. No empty praise like 地道/自然.
   - No trailing spaces, no blank lines, no invisible characters.
